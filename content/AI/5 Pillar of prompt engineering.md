1. **1.** **Direction:**
    
    Articulate what you want the AI to do as clearly as possible. Be specific about the task, the desired tone, and any relevant constraints.
    
2. **2.** **Format:**
    
    Tell the AI how you want the output to be structured. This could include the type of document, length, or specific formatting requirements.
    
3. **3.** **Examples:**
    
    Providing examples of what you want the AI to generate is crucial. This helps the AI understand the nuances of your request and reduces ambiguity.
    
4. **4.** **Evaluation:**
    
    Establish clear criteria for evaluating the AI's response. This could involve assessing accuracy, completeness, or adherence to specific guidelines.
    
5. **5.** **Divide Labor:**
    
    Breaking down complex tasks into smaller, manageable steps.
